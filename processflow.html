0. Checked Bag Process
The complete end-to-end workflow of checking luggage at an airport.
1. Terminal Arrival
The passenger arrives at the airport terminal with their luggage.
2. Check-in Desk
Passenger locates the specific airline check-in counter or self-service kiosk.
3. ID Verification
Airline staff or kiosk verifies the passenger's identity and booking details.
4. Bag Weighing
The luggage is placed on a scale to check against airline weight restrictions.
5. Tag Attachment
A destination-coded barcode tag is printed and securely attached to the bag handle.
6. Receipt Issuance
A baggage claim stub is provided to the passenger for tracking purposes.
7. Conveyor Induction
The bag is placed on the conveyor belt to enter the airport's internal system.
8. Security Screening
The bag passes through X-ray machines and explosive detection systems.
9. Sorting System
Automated scanners read the tag and route the bag to the specific flight's loading area.
10. Aircraft Loading
Ground handlers manually load the bag into the aircraft's cargo hold.
